Life Pillars Habit Tracking Explained

Life pillars habit tracking means grouping your routines by life area so you can see not just what you completed, but what kind of life your effort is creating.

What life pillars are

Common pillars include fitness, mindfulness, learning, relationships, sleep and work. Instead of holding every habit in a flat list, pillars show where each one belongs.

Why they matter

A daily check-in alone cannot tell you whether your life is balanced. You might have a strong completion rate while still ignoring whole areas that matter to you. Pillars reveal that problem quickly.

This connects strongly to behavioural analytics because once habits are grouped well, the app can show which parts of life are thriving and which are starving.

How to choose your pillars

Choose categories that reflect the life you actually want, not categories that only sound impressive. Keep them broad enough to be useful and few enough to stay readable. Sortyd uses life pillars to make progress easier to interpret at a glance.
