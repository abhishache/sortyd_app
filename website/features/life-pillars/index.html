Life Pillar Tracking: See Your Whole Life at a Glance

Know whether your effort is actually spread across the life you care about. Free on iOS, Sortyd groups habits into life pillars like fitness, learning, mindfulness and relationships so progress becomes easier to read.

Why pillars matter

A flat list of habits cannot show where your life is drifting. Pillars reveal whether you are over-investing in one area while ignoring another, which is what makes the tracking feel more human and less mechanical.

When a pillar starts sliding, the AI coach and analytics have better context for explaining the problem. You are not just missing tasks; you are underfeeding a part of your life.

A better way to review progress

Pillar completion percentages create an instant overview of balance, not just output. That helps you plan more thoughtfully across the week and notice patterns you would miss in a normal tracker.
