AI Habit Coach That Reads Your Actual Behaviour

Get advice that matches what you actually do, not generic reminders. Free on iOS, Sortyd reads your completion history to show where your habits break, where your momentum builds, and what small change is most likely to help next.

It reads patterns, not guesses

Sortyd looks at your completion data across days, times, streak breaks, and falling routines. That means the AI can tell you which days are strongest, which habits are cooling off, and whether your schedule is too crowded to succeed.

Instead of acting like a chatbot that waits for you to explain your life, it studies the tracker itself. That makes the coaching more specific, more useful, and far less repetitive.

Why it feels different from reminders

Reminders can tell you when to act. A real coach tells you why you are not acting and what to change next. Sortyd closes that gap by connecting coaching to streaks, life pillars, weekly planning, and long-term behaviour analytics.

If you want a free iPhone system that helps you build results instead of just collecting checkmarks, this is the feature that changes the whole app.
