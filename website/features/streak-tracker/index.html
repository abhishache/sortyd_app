Streak Tracker: Keep Your Habits Alive Day After Day

Keep consistency visible so habits feel worth protecting. Free on iOS, Sortyd's streak tracker shows your active runs, highlights risk early, and helps you recover quickly when real life breaks the chain.

Why streaks work

Streaks turn invisible discipline into something you can see. That visual momentum matters because people are more likely to repeat a behaviour when progress feels tangible.

Sortyd uses streaks as motivation, but it also puts them in context with analytics so you can tell whether a streak is strong or just fragile luck.

Missed a day? Learn from it

One broken day does not need to become a broken month. The app makes missed days visible, then connects them to patterns, reminders and coaching so recovery is practical instead of emotional.
