Habit Tracker for Mindfulness and Mental Wellbeing

Mindfulness habits are often the first to disappear when life gets crowded. Free on iOS, Sortyd helps you protect meditation, journalling, walking and sleep-support habits with gentle structure instead of pressure.

Why these habits fall off first

Mental wellbeing routines are easy to postpone because they rarely feel urgent in the moment. That makes them vulnerable to packed schedules and emotional fatigue. A tracker has to be calm enough to support the habit without becoming another source of guilt.

How Sortyd helps

Life pillars make it obvious when your wellbeing habits are shrinking. The AI coach can notice a decline early, while reminders and progress grades create just enough accountability to keep the routine alive.

Representative user feedback often lands on the same theme: the app feels supportive because it shows patterns clearly instead of shaming missed days.
