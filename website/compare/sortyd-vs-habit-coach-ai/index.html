The core difference

Coaching is strongest when it can read what you actually did, not just what you remember or choose to type in a chat. Sortyd keeps the data and the coaching in the same place, which makes the advice more grounded and less abstract.

That is the reason users who want real behavioural feedback tend to prefer a combined system over a chat-first coach.
