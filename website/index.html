The Problem

Most habit apps were built for the wrong brain.

They punish missed days. They bury you in features. They turn self-improvement into another thing to fail at.

🧠

ADHD

Working memory and executive function deficits make rigid to-do lists useless. You need visual weight, immediate feedback, and zero cognitive load.

💭

Anxiety

All-or-nothing thinking turns one missed day into a full abandonment. You need a system that says "40% is great, keep going" — not silence.

🔄

Bad Habits

Bad habits are coping mechanisms, not character flaws. Replacing them means giving your brain a competing loop that's nearly as satisfying.
