Sortyd combines daily habit tracking, a flexible to-do list, weekly planning, life-area tracking and AI coaching so your tasks, routines and goals live in one place.

Habit tracker app

Build streaks, monitor completion rates and understand which routines are actually sticking.

To-do list app

Capture one-off tasks with clear reminders, separate from recurring daily habits.

Weekly planner

Plan your week around priorities instead of reacting to scattered tasks and reminders.

AI coaching

Get personalized feedback from your real behaviour patterns, not generic productivity advice.
